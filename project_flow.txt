===================================================================
TESEPR BOOK / TRAVEL AGENCY ERP - PROJECT FLOW (01-Sep to 18-Sep)
===================================================================

OVERALL FLOW
------------
Prototype Analysis  ->  UI Design  ->  Python Logic (Django Backend)
   ->  DB Connection (SQL Server)  ->  Page-by-Page Integration & Testing

-------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 1: PROTOTYPE ANALYSIS (01-Sep to 03-Sep)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Day 1 (01-Sep) - Requirement study: travel agency accounting workflow
                 (Airline / Hotel / Bus / Train / Tour / Visa / Insurance
                 booking, cancellation, reschedule), identified core
                 modules: Masters, Transactions, Accounts, Reports.
Day 2 (02-Sep) - Mapped accounting requirements to standard double-entry
                 structure: Ledger Groups, Ledgers, Voucher Types,
                 Journal Voucher rules per transaction type.
Day 3 (03-Sep) - Finalized module list and page inventory (list below),
                 decided tech stack: Django 5.x + mssql-django/pyodbc
                 backend, static HTML/JS frontend (no SPA framework),
                 shared topnav/shell (shell.js) across all pages.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 2: UI DESIGN (04-Sep to 08-Sep)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Day 4 (04-Sep) - Shell/topnav design: logo, company/flag switcher,
                 notifications, theme toggle, menu bar (dashboard.html,
                 shell.js skeleton).
Day 5 (05-Sep) - Masters UI: Ledger Groups (groups.html), Chart of
                 Accounts (accounts.html), Customers/Suppliers
                 (customers.html, suppliers.html, supplier-master.html).
Day 6 (06-Sep) - Masters UI continued: Company Master, FOP Master,
                 PG Master, Master Mapping, Voucher Type
                 (company-master.html, fop-master.html, pg-master.html,
                 master-mapping.html, voucher-type.html).
Day 7 (07-Sep) - Transaction entry screens layout: Ticket Entry (Airline
                 booking/cancellation/reschedule/SSR), Hotel, Bus, Train,
                 Tour, Visa, Insurance, Misc entry forms.
Day 8 (08-Sep) - Reports UI layout: Day Book, Ledger Book, Trial Balance,
                 Cash & Bank Book, Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, GST
                 (GSTR-1/2B/3B), TDS, DSR Airline Booking.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 3: PYTHON LOGIC (DJANGO BACKEND) (09-Sep to 13-Sep)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Day 9  (09-Sep) - Django project scaffolding (backend/accounting app),
                  models for Ledger Groups, Ledgers, Voucher Types,
                  Company Master, FOP/PG Master.
Day 10 (10-Sep) - Ticket/TicketLine models + Journal Voucher posting
                  logic (JV_LINE_MAP, auto category/prefix numbering).
Day 11 (11-Sep) - Reschedule flow logic: RescheduleAirlineTicket /
                  RescheduleAirlineTicketLine models, reschedule-only JV
                  rules, eligibility/chain-link handling.
Day 12 (12-Sep) - Reports logic: Day Book, Ledger Book, Trial Balance,
                  Cash & Bank Book, Monthly Summary - ledger balance/
                  transaction aggregation functions.
Day 13 (13-Sep) - GST/TDS/Balance Sheet/Profit & Loss report logic,
                  Invoice Number auto-numbering (Voucher Type Number
                  Method: Manual / Automatic / Automatic & Manual
                  Override).

-------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 4: DB CONNECTION (SQL SERVER) (14-Sep to 15-Sep)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Day 14 (14-Sep) - mssql-django/pyodbc connection setup, initial
                  migrations for Masters (Ledger Groups, Ledgers,
                  Company Master, FOP/PG Master, Voucher Type).
Day 15 (15-Sep) - Migrations for Tickets/TicketLines, Journal Voucher,
                  Reschedule tables; Schema.sql mirrored to match every
                  migration (tables, FKs, indexes, constraints).

-------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 5: PAGE-BY-PAGE INTEGRATION & TESTING (16-Sep to 18-Sep)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Day 16 (16-Sep) - Wired Masters pages to live APIs (Ledger Groups,
                  Accounts, Customers, Suppliers, Company Master, FOP/PG
                  Master, Master Mapping, Voucher Type, User Management).
Day 17 (17-Sep) - Wired Transaction pages to live APIs (Ticket Entry +
                  Airline Reschedule/Cancellation/SSR, Hotel, Bus, Train,
                  Tour, Visa, Insurance, Misc) end to end with save/edit/
                  view flows.
Day 18 (18-Sep) - Wired Reports pages to live APIs (Day Book, Ledger
                  Book, Trial Balance, Cash & Bank Book, Ledger Monthly,
                  Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, GSTR-1/1-Recon/2B-Recon/
                  3B, TDS, DSR Airline Booking) and full end-to-end
                  regression pass across all pages below.

           shared shell.js for topnav/menu/company-flag across pages

           endpoints (/api/...), no SPA framework
